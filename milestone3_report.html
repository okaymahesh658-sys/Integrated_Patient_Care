Milestone 3 Overview

Milestone 3 focuses on developing advanced features that help healthcare professionals efficiently retrieve, analyze, and manage patient information. In this milestone, interns will implement a Patient Search Module to quickly locate patient records using various search criteria, generate detailed Medical Reports containing consultation, prescription, laboratory, and medical history details, and build an Analytics Dashboard to display hospital statistics through charts and reports.

Additionally, interns will develop a Notification & Reminder System for appointments, laboratory results, and follow-up visits, along with report export functionality (PDF/Excel). By the end of this milestone, all these modules will be integrated and tested to provide a complete reporting and decision-support system for the Integrated Patient Care Management System, improving the efficiency, accuracy, and quality of patient care.

Key Modules Developed:

  • Day 1 – Patient Search Module: Search patient records by ID, Name, Phone, or Aadhaar.
  • Day 2 – Pharmacy Management: Manage medicine inventory, stock levels, and dispensing.
  • Day 3 – Billing & Payment: Calculate hospital fees, record transactions, and generate invoices.
  • Day 4 – REST API Development: Secure CRUD API endpoints with response time <300ms (95th %ile).
  • Day 5 – Notification Management: Multi-channel alerts with >95% delivery success rate.
  • Day 6 – Dashboard Analytics & Security: Real-time statistics, graphical charts, and RBAC security.
  • Day 7 – System Integration & Performance: End-to-end integration and system health optimization.
